What we do

If it’s upholstered, it belongs on the bench.

From a single worn barstool to a full built-in banquette or a set of boat cushions — Gemma re-covers, rebuilds and refreshes the pieces you’re not ready to part with.

Sofas & couches

Full reupholstery and rebuilt cushions for sofas, sleeper sofas and sectionals — made new in fresh fabric with crisp attention to detail.

Dining chairs & barstools

Sets recovered and done beautifully — from six matching barstools to well-loved dining chairs that come back looking amazing.

Cushions & banquettes

Custom cushions built from scratch for built-in banquettes, breakfast nooks, benches and window seats — made to fit your space exactly.

Boat & marine seating

Boat cushions, helm and leaning-post seats, and covers rebuilt to handle the elements — including cushions recreated better than the originals after a storm.

Repairs & patching

A small hole or worn spot doesn’t mean the end of a favorite piece — careful repairs and patching keep your sectional or chair going for years.

Fabric selection

Honest guidance choosing the right fabric — a faithful match to something you love, a sturdier weave, or the perfect new look for the room.
